    The framework

    An idea is easy.

    Getting it into the world takes a system. Mine has six letters.

    FIXTRS

    1. Match your focus to a format.

    2. Social impact fits any industry, as long as you keep your intention.

    3. Extract. Then execute.

    4. Try it. Test it.

    5. Use every available resource. Relationships especially.

    6. Then share it.

    You can apply this to any project.
